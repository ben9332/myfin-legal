MyFin — Privacy Policy

MyFin is a private, self-hosted personal finance application. It is operated by its owner for the owner’s own use. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

What data the app accesses

When the owner connects a QuickBooks Online company that they have been authorized to access, MyFin reads accounting data from that company through the Intuit API: customer and job names, invoices, bills, expenses, payments, and files attached to those transactions. Access is read-only; MyFin does not create or change records in QuickBooks. Only data belonging to the project the owner has confirmed is retrieved and kept.

How the data is used

The data is used solely to reconcile what was billed on the owner’s project against payments made and supporting documents. It is not used for advertising, profiling, or any other purpose.

Storage and security

Data is stored on a server owned and controlled by the owner. The server is not reachable from the public internet. QuickBooks access tokens are encrypted at rest. Access to the application requires a login.

Sharing

Data obtained from QuickBooks is not sold, rented, or shared with any third party.

Retention and deletion

Data is kept for as long as the owner needs it for the project’s records. The owner can disconnect QuickBooks at any time, which revokes the app’s access, and can delete the stored data.
